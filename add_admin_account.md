# Adding admin@sphereemr.com to Xcode

## Step 1: Add the Account

1. In Xcode, go to **Xcode > Settings** (or press `Cmd+,`)
2. Click the **Accounts** tab
3. Click the **+** button at the bottom left
4. Select **Apple ID**
5. Enter: `admin@sphereemr.com`
6. Enter the password
7. Click **Sign In**

## Step 2: Find the Team ID

After signing in:
1. You'll see `admin@sphereemr.com` in the accounts list
2. Click on it
3. On the right side, you'll see:
   - Team name
   - **Team ID** (this is what we need!)

The Team ID will look like: `ABC123XYZ` or `44GY3MRW88`
