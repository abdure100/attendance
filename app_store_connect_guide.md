# How to Access App Store Connect

## Direct Link:
https://appstoreconnect.apple.com

## Steps:

### 1. Open App Store Connect
- Go to: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
- Or click the link above (should open in your browser)

### 2. Sign In
- Sign in with: `admin@sphereemr.com`
- Use your Apple ID password

### 3. Navigate to Your App
- Once signed in, you'll see the dashboard
- Click **"My Apps"** in the top navigation
- Look for your app or create a new one

### 4. Create New App (if needed)
- Click the **"+"** button (top left)
- Select **"New App"**
- Fill in:
  - Platform: iOS
  - Name: **SphereEMR Pro**
  - Primary Language: English
  - Bundle ID: Select `com.sphereemr.attendance`
  - SKU: (can be anything, like "sphereemr-pro-001")
- Click **"Create"**

### 5. Upload to TestFlight
- After creating the app, go to the **TestFlight** tab
- Wait for your upload from Xcode to appear
- Processing takes 10-30 minutes

## Quick Access:
- Bookmark: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
- Or search "App Store Connect" in your browser
