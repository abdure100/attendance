# Do Internal Testers Get Emails?

## Yes! ✅

When you add an internal tester in App Store Connect:

1. **They receive an email invitation automatically**
   - Email comes from: `noreply@email.apple.com`
   - Subject: "You've been invited to test [App Name]"
   - Sent immediately when you add them

2. **What the email contains:**
   - Invitation to test your app
   - Link to accept the invitation
   - Instructions to install TestFlight app
   - Your app name: "Attendance & Tripsheet"

3. **Email timing:**
   - Sent immediately when you click "Add"
   - Usually arrives within a few minutes
   - Check spam/junk folder if not received

## What Testers Need to Do:

1. **Check their email** for the invitation
2. **Click the link** in the email (or use the redemption code)
3. **Install TestFlight app** from App Store (if not already installed)
4. **Open TestFlight app**
5. **Accept the invitation**
6. **Install your app** and start testing

## Important Notes:

- **Email is sent automatically** - You don't need to do anything extra
- **Testers must have TestFlight app** installed on their device
- **Email might go to spam** - Tell testers to check spam folder
- **Invitation doesn't expire** - They can accept anytime
- **Multiple testers** - Each gets their own email

## If Tester Doesn't Receive Email:

1. **Check spam/junk folder**
2. **Verify email address is correct**
3. **Make sure build is added to the test group**
4. **Check App Store Connect** - Tester status should show "Invited"
5. **Resend invitation** if needed (click on tester → Resend Invitation)

## Alternative: Share Redemption Code

If email doesn't work, you can:
1. Go to the test group
2. Click on a tester
3. Copy the **redemption code**
4. Share it with the tester manually
5. They can redeem it in TestFlight app
